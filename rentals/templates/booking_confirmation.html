What Happens Next

The reservation is now inside the staff workflow. These are the next live steps in the system.

1. Staff Prepare Handover

The booking moves into the paid and confirmed queue so the team can prepare the vehicle for release.

2. Pick-up or Delivery

The agreed route, service type, and any extras stay attached to the booking for the actual handover.

3. Return Inspection

After the trip, staff check the vehicle back in, review its condition, and settle any final charges if needed.

4. Booking Closure

Once the return is complete, the booking is closed and the vehicle moves back into the correct fleet state.
